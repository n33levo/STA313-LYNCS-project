# Development plan

We will use R to prepare the school records and the OW and ODSP series, check missing values and assign school coordinates to the 2021 metropolitan boundaries. ggplot2 will help us compare figure designs; jsonlite will export the checked values for the webpage. The first analysis pairs each school year with March of its ending year and keeps the two programmes separate. We will build the page in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, using D3 for the map and charts. Selecting an area will update its history, family composition and school list; school filters will not change assistance caseloads. One member will lead the R analysis, layout, webpage implementation and GitHub Pages setup. Two members will support data checks and review the numerical examples. Design and writing responsibilities will be shared: one member will refine the interface and instructions, another will explain the design choices, and another will check the illustrations and assemble feedback. These reviews will inform the next version rather than wait until the final submission. We aim to complete the linked webpage by 25 November, test its published version by 28 November and finish interface checks by 30 November. The remaining group work will cover the demo script, recording and editing before the 7 December deadline. Before publication, we will check keyboard controls, narrow screens, missing-data states and agreement with the R results. Population rates will be added only if compatible boundaries and reference dates can be established.
