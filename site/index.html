A note on interpretation

School locations and assistance cases share a geography. These files do not link pupils to cases or measure whether an area receives enough support.

Sources & coverage

Course data: school information, Ontario Works and Ontario Disability Support Program CMA files. School codes: Deaf, Blind, Deaf/Blind and Demonstration.

Statistics Canada 2021 CMA boundaries are reference geography. Historical assistance boundaries may differ. Ottawa-Gatineau uses the Ontario portion. Population rates remain pending verification.

School years pair with March of their ending year. The historical chart shows all 93 quarters. Belleville is outside these assistance files, and records without coordinates remain in the school list.
